## Changes

1. **Render the authoritative offer message**
   - Treat every `offer_*` notification as a pre-built message in the Alerts screen.
   - Display the exact role-aware message generated by the offers backend instead of generic buyer-only fallback text.
   - Keep safe fallback copy only for legacy rows that have no message.

2. **Make fallback copy role-neutral**
   - Replace misleading offer fallbacks with wording that cannot claim the viewer is the buyer or seller when that context is unavailable.
   - Preserve the current username emphasis, listing image and offer emoji styling.

3. **Correct offer notification destinations**
   - Buyer offer accepted or declined -> Offers, Sent.
   - Seller-sent offer accepted or declined -> Offers, Sent.
   - New offer, counter-offer or seller discount received -> Offers, Received.
   - Seller auto-accept notification -> Offers, Received (currently incorrectly classified as Sent).
   - Keep push-tap and in-app alert taps on the same routing path.

4. **Verify the complete notification matrix**
   - Check buyer-to-seller offer creation, seller-to-buyer special offer, counter-offers in both directions, manual acceptance, automatic acceptance, decline and withdrawal/expiry behavior.
   - Confirm recipient, actor username, message, push payload and destination tab for each notifying transition.
   - Confirm accepted offers still tell only the buyer to pay within 24 hours, while the seller is told the buyer accepted and has 24 hours to pay.

## Technical scope

- Update the Alerts message-selection and offer-routing logic in the frontend.
- Adjust backend offer notification copy only if the matrix verification finds a role mismatch in a generated message.
- Do not change offer pricing, acceptance, cart, checkout or payment rules.
